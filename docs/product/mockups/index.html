A door to somewhere else

Vrata puts a virtual door into a real room. Walk through it and you are standing in another place.

Scan the room, place the door

Walk around the room for about two minutes, then put the door where you want it. You do this once.

Visitors just open the app

The door comes from the server and stands in the same place for everyone. No markers, and nothing for a visitor to set up.

Vrata needs your camera

The camera shows the room and keeps the door in its place.

When you scan a room, a short video of it goes to the Vrata server on this Wi‑Fi. It is used only to build the room map.
Nothing leaves this network. No account, no ads.
You can delete a room and its video at any time.

Can’t reach the server

“Front desk PC” is not answering. Your phone and the server must be on the same Wi‑Fi.

The door needs the server only to find its place. A door that is already standing keeps working.

The camera is off

Vrata can’t show a door without the camera. Allow it in the phone settings.

This scan didn’t work

The server could not build a map from it. This happens when the walk was too short or too fast, or the walls are very plain.

Walk for at least a minute, slowly, and pass every wall.

This phone can’t show AR

Vrata needs Google Play Services for AR, and this phone does not support it.

Lost track of the roomMove the phone slowly over furniture or pictures.

This doesn’t look like Seminar room B

Vrata could not match what the camera sees to the scan. If the furniture or the light has changed a lot, a new scan helps.
