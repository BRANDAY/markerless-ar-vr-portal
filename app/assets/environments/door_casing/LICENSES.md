# Door casing and door leaf — credits and licences

Files in this folder: `casing_*.obj`, `portal_window.obj`, `door_leaf.obj`, `door_casing.jpg`,
`door_leaf.jpg`, `portal_window.png`.

## Models

The models (casing profile, mitred corners, threshold, plank door with strap hinges and ring handle)
were generated for this project by `build/build_door.py`. No third-party geometry is used.

## Textures

Both textures are painted by the build script from two wood photographs of Poly Haven, licence
**CC0 1.0** (public domain, https://polyhaven.com/license):

- **"European Walnut Veneer 04"** by Jenelle van Heerden —
  https://polyhaven.com/a/european_walnut_veneer_04 (the casing).
- **"Oak Veneer 01"** by Jenelle van Heerden — https://polyhaven.com/a/oak_veneer_01 (the door leaf).

Downloaded on 2026-11-20 (2K diffuse JPEG). The photographs were stained darker, cut into strips and
planks, and the shading of the mouldings, the grooves and the ironwork was painted over them. The
ironwork itself is drawn by the script.

CC0 does not require attribution; it is given here as a courtesy.
